# Buddy Town diary — deferred
This is a FUTURE Buddy Town design/template, explicitly outside hackathon acceptance. It does not add a watcher, background learning job or model training.

Each runtime keeps a private lessons-learned diary. At a mission boundary, write an append-only entry using templates/diary.md: what happened, evidence, why it happened, what changed, when the lesson applies, counterexamples, and a proposed future check. Include mistakes and failed approaches without blame or invented certainty.

Use one new file/comment per entry, owned by that runtime. Suggested filename: YYYY-MM-DD_HH-MM-SS_UTC_Runtime_bootID_entryID.md. Never edit another agent's diary or silently rewrite history; correct with a linked superseding entry. Avoid credentials, personal sensitive data and copied private conversations.

A diary is a reflection source, not an authoritative policy. Separate observation from hypothesis. Before promoting a lesson into the shared charter or a procedure, check the original evidence, current relevance and counterexamples; obtain independent review and the appropriate authority. Preserve links between the lesson and the resulting revision.

On future startup, consult the current reviewed lesson index for the active task. Do not inject an unbounded historical diary into every prompt. Expire or supersede environment-specific lessons when conditions change. Improvement means better retrieved instructions, checklists and behavior; it does not claim that the underlying model retrains itself or changes weights.

Future acceptance: two sessions can retrieve a relevant lesson, identify its limits, apply the resulting check, and retain corrections across restart. Implementation and live proof are deferred to Buddy Town.
